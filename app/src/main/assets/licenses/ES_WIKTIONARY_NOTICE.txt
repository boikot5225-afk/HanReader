Spanish dictionary (es/dict.tsv, es/forms.tsv, es/grammar.tsv), built by tools/build_es_dict.py from:
- Russian Wiktionary (ru.wiktionary.org), Spanish words: Russian meanings, via the wiktextract
  extract published at https://kaikki.org/ruwiktionary/ (downloaded November 2026);
- English Wiktionary (en.wiktionary.org), Spanish words: English meanings and word forms, via
  https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Spanish/ (downloaded November 2026).

Word frequency (the rank column of es/dict.tsv): FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (content/2018/es/es_50k.txt), built from OpenSubtitles 2018;
content licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

Russian meanings marked as machine translation: English Wiktionary meanings translated into Russian
with the DeepSeek API (tools/translate_es_ru.py, data/es/ru_ai.jsonl), not checked by a lexicographer;
distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 as a derivative of Wiktionary.

Edited meanings (source "r"): for the most frequent words, the Russian meanings (Wiktionary or machine
translation) reordered by use and given a main meaning by the DeepSeek API (tools/refine_es_ru.py,
data/es/ru_refined.jsonl); distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Regional and style labels (where a sense is used): from the English Wiktionary sense tags; the Russian
senses of words with such senses were labelled and reordered by the DeepSeek API
(tools/refine_es_ru.py --regional, data/es/ru_regional.jsonl); distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
