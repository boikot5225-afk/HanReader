Fonts bundled in res/font, subset to the characters HanReader needs (GB2312, HSK lists,
frequent dictionary characters; Latin, pinyin and Cyrillic for the UI fonts).
All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt in this folder).

- Noto Serif SC, Noto Sans SC — Google / Adobe (Source Han), google/fonts
- LXGW WenKai (霞鹜文楷) — LXGW, npm @fontpkg/lxgw-wen-kai 1.520.0
- Zhuque Fangsong (朱雀仿宋) technical preview 0.212 — TrionesType, npm @fontpkg/zhuque-fangsong-technical-preview
- Oswald — Vernon Adams et al., google/fonts
- IBM Plex Mono — IBM, google/fonts
- Cormorant Garamond — Christian Thalmann, google/fonts
- Inter — Rasmus Andersson, google/fonts

Reserved Font Names (where declared) are not used for the subsets' own names beyond the original
family names kept in the font files; the fonts are not sold on their own.
